01 · About me

From experimentation
to production.

I am an AI / Machine Learning Engineer focused on building and industrializing robust AI systems, from experimentation to production. Based in Paris.

At Framatome, I worked on the end-to-end development of an industrial computer vision system for real-time zirconium defect detection, from dataset labeling and YOLOv8 training to an embedded iPad application used by field experts on the production line. I also conducted R&D on diffusion models for synthetic defect generation, and on generative AI, Transformers and LLMs.

I have a strong background in applied mathematics, optimization, statistical modeling and deep learning, with a Double Master's Degree in Statistical Engineering & Data Science from ISUP & Sorbonne University.

I enjoy translating complex technical and operational constraints into scalable, reliable AI solutions, with a strong focus on measurable performance and real-world impact. I'm especially interested in high-impact AI applications across healthcare, security, finance and decision systems.
